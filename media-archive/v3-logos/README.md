# Guild logos, as supplied

The originals behind `logo-stunt-guild.png` and `logo-stunt-register-ireland.png`
in the theme.

Both arrived as JPEGs with backgrounds that could not sit on a dark page: the Stunt
Guild as a black tile inside a white surround, and Stunt Register Ireland as a phone
screenshot with an iOS live-text button burned into the bottom right corner.

They were converted to transparent PNGs by knocking the background out on a canvas,
masking the button, and auto-cropping to the artwork. If either guild supplies a proper
vector or transparent asset, prefer it over these.
